# Establishing the Outpost

Welcome to the first entry of my digital journal. This space isn't just a portfolio; it's a living archive of experiments, travels, and technical discoveries.

## The Architecture
To keep this space minimal yet powerful, I've built it on a **Cloudflare-native** stack:
*   **Astro** for the frontend engine.
*   **Cloudflare D1** for metadata and lightning-fast indexing.
*   **Cloudflare R2** for storing these very words and the images from my travels.

## Why an Outpost?
While many build "digital gardens," I chose the concept of an **Outpost**. A garden implies constant pruning and gentle growth. An outpost feels more intentional—a base established at the frontier of exploration. 

This is where I'll report back from technical deep-dives into software architecture or share coordinates and memories from geographic wanderings.
